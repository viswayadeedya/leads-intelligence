# Differentiation must be earned
Hypothesis: compared with generic local agency audits, evidence-backed customer-journey investigations will produce more relevant owner conversations. This is unproven.

## Competitor reality check
Before claiming novelty, sample at least 10 local agency offers and record whether they provide: website audits, local SEO/AI visibility, social listening, evidence links, journey-level investigation, and human verification. Record URLs and dates; do not infer 'nobody does this'.

## Kill criteria and pivots
After 20 real investigations, measure % with at least one independently verifiable, actionable finding; median research minutes per accepted finding; false-positive rate; spend per accepted finding. After outreach to a small approved set, track reply/diagnostic interest. If findings are generic or frequently false, narrow to one journey (e.g., after-hours contact) and improve quality before scaling. If owners do not care, interview them before adding integrations.

## Value ladder
Evidence → relevant owner question → optional diagnostic → consented deeper audit → appropriate service. Never force an AI receptionist as the solution to every problem.
