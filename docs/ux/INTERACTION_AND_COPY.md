# UX writing and interaction states
Voice: concise, calm, precise, helpful. No marketing hype inside the operator UI.

Good: 'Website checked at 10:42 AM. No appointment link was found on the 5 pages inspected.'
Bad: 'Business is losing leads because its booking is broken.'
Good: 'Reddit discussion identified. Content not automatically collected; review source manually.'
Bad: 'Social sentiment: 27% negative' without permitted representative evidence.
Good: '3 of 5 sources checked; Facebook access restricted. Report can continue with limitations.'
Bad: 'All research complete.'

## Confirmation rules
Deleting evidence: confirm and record audit event. Approving owner preview: show exact content, source visibility and redaction summary. Copying an outreach draft: allow immediately, log draft copied not sent. Credentials: masked, test connection only server-side. No silent mode switch mock→live.

## Usability tests (perform manually or scripted browser)
1. Start mock investigation in under 60 seconds with no instructions.
2. Find original evidence behind any report claim in at most two interactions.
3. Identify restricted source and understand what it does *not* mean.
4. Reject a false claim; confirm report changes and audit trail updates.
5. Preview owner-safe report; verify restricted source material is absent.
Capture screenshots, issues, and fixes in BUILD_LOG. These are goals, not claims of measured performance.
