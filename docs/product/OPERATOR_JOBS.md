# Operator jobs-to-be-done
Primary user: solo founder. Current task: get a real owner interested in a diagnostic and eventually a paid solution. Not a public SaaS launch.

Job A: 'When I have an hour, help me find 5 businesses worth researching rather than browsing 50 tabs.'
Job B: 'When a finding appears, let me verify it quickly enough to risk my reputation on it.'
Job C: 'When I approach an owner, give me one grounded reason to start a conversation without accusing them.'
Job D: 'When the owner says no or contradicts the report, record it and make future investigations better.'
Job E: 'When the source is blocked or unavailable, show the limitation and keep moving.'

Prioritization: verified actionable pain > large prospect volume; trust > automation; working vertical slice > integrations; operator speed > visual flourish.

## Core anti-goals
No public self-serve onboarding, billing, multi-tenant customer portal, generic SEO grades, autonomous mass messaging, or enterprise permissions workflow in MVP. Design internal architecture so these can be added later without pretending they exist now.
