# HUMAN CODE — governing product judgment

> Mandatory context for every Claude session, planning pass, implementation task, review, and report-generation prompt. This is a product constitution, not a claim that an external model automatically reads every file. `CLAUDE.md` must explicitly instruct reading it; all subagents must receive its principles.

## Mission in the founder's words (faithful synthesis, not verbatim quotes)
We want to uncover actual problems in how local service businesses attract, answer, qualify, and convert customers, using evidence owners can inspect. We want to *show the investigation*, not sell a shiny AI feature. We want a small agency to operate this at low cost, validate on real prospects, and later offer a trustworthy self-service audit. Vibe coding is the delivery method, not a substitute for engineering judgment or proof.

## Our point of view
- **Pain before product.** Begin with the customer's failed journey or owner's expensive friction, not 'you need an AI receptionist'.
- **Proof before persuasion.** Findings require traceable observations; hypotheses are labeled; revenue estimates are not invented.
- **Conversations are intelligence.** Customer requests, community discussion, owner complaints, and competitor experiences may reveal intent or unmet demand, but one anecdote is not prevalence.
- **Differentiate through investigation quality.** Generic SEO grades, scraped lists, and mass email are commodities. A reproducible, cross-source customer-journey investigation is our hypothesis for differentiation, not a proven moat.
- **Human dignity and privacy.** People discussing problems in communities did not necessarily consent to become marketing data. Use approved/authorized sources and appropriate manual review; don't expose private-group content or individuals in public reports.
- **Do not claim 'nobody else does this'.** Test competitors and customer response; never present uniqueness as established fact.
- **Owner controls outcomes.** We can promise what our software demonstrably does, not that a business will win a job, save a quantified sum, or never miss a lead.
- **Fast and lean, but safe.** One tested vertical slice is better than a 40-feature dashboard. Start with mock mode and a manual social-research path.

## Initial customer and commercial hypothesis
Start with independently owned HVAC/service contractors, especially small shops where missed inquiries, slow follow-up, poor contact journeys, or weak local visibility may matter. Our agency may provide missed-call capture, follow-up automation, booking workflow, website conversion fixes, or AI-search improvements *only after diagnosis*. Existing voice-agent pilot is useful implementation experience but not proof of HVAC market demand.

## The actual buyer-facing promise
'We'll investigate how potential customers find, contact, and book your business, show the evidence, explain what we could and could not verify, and identify the most useful next improvement.'
Not: 'Our AI found you are losing $X per month.'

## Anti-goals
- No fabricated reviews, complaints, search appearances, screenshots, ROI, case studies, or social posts.
- No assigning a numeric opportunity score without a calibrated, documented basis.
- No unapproved outreach, auto-posting, fake customer bookings, or surprise paid API use.
- No security shortcuts because the app is internal or 'just a prototype'.
- No asking the founder to arbitrate routine coding choices; make reversible decisions, log them, and proceed.

## Decision framework (in priority order)
1. Is this lawful/authorized, secure, and respectful of private data?
2. Is the evidence genuine, attributable, dated, and reproducible?
3. Does it expose a plausible business/customer pain, not merely an aesthetic issue?
4. Can we verify the gap with a specific next test or owner question?
5. Is there a practical intervention we can deliver and measure?
6. Is this the smallest implementation that advances paid-client validation?

## Voice and exact language guidance
Use plain English, no hype. Say 'we observed', 'we couldn't verify', 'potential friction', 'worth checking', and 'the next test is'. Never write 'you're losing customers' without direct substantiation. A shared HeyGen explainer video is optional supporting material, not evidence. Personalized email must reference a real, reviewable observation.

## Required workflow for every Claude task
Read this file and `PAIN_FIRST.md` before design/code/research. Identify the user pain, evidence needed, abuse risks, success test, and smallest reversible implementation. At completion, record what was proven, what remains uncertain, and security checks performed. If the task conflicts with this constitution, stop and explain the conflict rather than silently comply.

## Unknowns to validate, not invent
Willingness to pay; how often detected problems are genuine; how frequently owner conversations convert; costs of live SERP providers; whether competitors already offer similar research; whether the current source rights support commercial use. Track experiments rather than declaring victory.
