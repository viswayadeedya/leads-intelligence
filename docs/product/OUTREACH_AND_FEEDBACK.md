# Outreach and Learning Loop

## Outreach workflow
Generate draft from approved opportunity cards only → human review → optional shared HeyGen explainer video link → booking link → explicit send approval → record response/outcome. MVP never sends automatically.

## Email constraints
Mention only confirmed observations. No fake familiarity, fake client results, fake urgency, unsupported 'lost jobs' claims, or fabricated metrics. Keep personalized evidence short and link to a reviewed report when appropriate. Respect applicable marketing-email laws and opt-outs.

## Learning loop
Record: delivered, reply, interested, meeting booked, diagnostic completed, paid pilot, disqualified, objection, no response. Keep outreach copy versions for experiments. Don't attribute causation to tiny samples.

## Offer framing
Offer to inspect a specific customer journey and discuss a possible fix. A pilot voice assistant may be relevant, but never force that solution when another intervention is better.
