# Product Brief

## Problem
Small AI agencies waste time pitching generic automation to unqualified businesses. Public signals are scattered across search results, business websites, directories, community conversations, and booking/contact experiences. Generic audits turn weak signals into confident but unsubstantiated sales claims.

## Product promise
Find specific, defensible opportunities for local service businesses and explain the observation, source, confidence, unknowns, owner verification question, and possible fix.

## Initial ICP
Independent HVAC companies, especially small operators (roughly 1–10 trucks) in Michigan. Future expansion: plumbing, electrical, roofing, dental, other service niches. Do not assume an HVAC business needs AI or has spare capacity.

## Agency service fit
Missed-call capture, call qualification, owner alerts, lead follow-up, appointment request flows, and other customer acquisition/operational fixes. Sell the smallest solution that fits verified pain.

## Primary user
One agency operator who investigates prospects, validates findings, reaches owners, and offers a free diagnostic. Secondary user later: business owner viewing a consented self-service report.

## Core hypothesis
An evidence-backed, context-specific diagnostic earns more relevant owner conversations than generic AI outreach. This is an unproven hypothesis to test, not a market fact.

## MVP metrics
- Time per completed investigation; variable cost per investigation
- % businesses correctly matched to sources
- % report claims with valid provenance
- % findings accepted by human reviewer
- % reports with at least one defensible and relevant opportunity
- Outreach replies, owner conversations, booked diagnostics, paid pilots

## Pilot target
Investigate 20 real companies, aim for 5+ credible opportunities and at least 1 interested owner. Targets are experiments, not guarantees. Compare against a simple manual-research baseline if possible.

## Product differentiator
Journey-oriented, multi-source evidence and explicit uncertainty, not source count, generic scoring, or scraping volume.
