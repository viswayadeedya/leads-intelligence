# Personas and Jobs

## Agency operator
When evaluating a local business, I want to find a verifiable customer acquisition or operational gap quickly, so I can start a relevant conversation without making unsupported claims.

## HVAC owner
When someone approaches me with a supposed problem, I want to see exactly what they observed, understand the uncertainty, and know what action is being proposed without exaggerated ROI claims.

## Customer journey lens
Potential customer: search → compare → evaluate trust → visit website → call/form/booking → response/follow-up. Research only observable parts; do not infer internal conversion outcomes from surface signals.

## Jobs and anti-jobs
- Job: find a serviceable, fixable problem with owner relevance
- Job: distinguish business pain from random internet negativity
- Job: document what must be verified by asking the owner
- Anti-job: maximize leads regardless of fit
- Anti-job: invent a dollar figure for missed revenue
- Anti-job: infer a single comment represents common customer experience
