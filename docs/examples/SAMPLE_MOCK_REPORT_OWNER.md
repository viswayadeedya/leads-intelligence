## Findings

### 1. No online request path found on inspected pages

**We observed:** On the 3 pages inspected on 2026-11-18, a phone number is listed, and no contact form, booking link or after-hours statement was found.

**Why it might matter (hypothesis):** A visitor who prefers not to call, or who visits outside office hours, may not find a way to request service online. Whether that matters depends on how calls are handled.

**Other explanations:**
- The business may deliberately prefer phone contact.
- A booking tool may exist on a page that was not inspected, or may load through JavaScript, which this check does not see.
- Calls outside office hours may already be answered by a person or an answering service.

**Question for you:** When someone tries to reach you outside office hours, how is the request captured and who follows up?

**Possible improvement:** Only if the owner confirms a gap: add a simple online request form or after-hours call capture with owner notifications.

**Confidence in the observation:** medium (this is not an estimate of business impact)
