# Search Recipes and Query Templates

These are research query patterns, not evidence by themselves. Use provider-supported search interfaces and record exact query, date, and geographic context.

## Company discovery
- `HVAC repair Grand Rapids MI`
- `furnace repair Lansing MI`
- `emergency AC repair Kalamazoo MI`
Filter actual service area, not just city string. Deduplicate by provider ID, verified domain, and address.

## Customer conversations
- `site:reddit.com "furnace repair" "never called back" Michigan`
- `site:reddit.com "HVAC" "appointment" "no show"`
- `site:facebook.com "[business name]" "appointment"`
- `"[business name]" "called back"`
These queries may surface unrelated companies, outdated stories, or missing context. Never attribute until verified.

## Owner pain
- `site:reddit.com/r/hvac "missed calls"`
- `site:reddit.com "HVAC owner" "answering service"`
- `"HVAC" "AI receptionist" "booking"`
Separate general market insight from evidence about a specific company.

## Competitor journey
For each verified competitor: compare visible emergency contact paths, forms, service descriptions, mobile usability, and booking requests. Do not infer actual booking rates.

## AI visibility
Use a stable local query set, e.g. `emergency furnace repair in [city]`, `AC repair [city]`, `HVAC maintenance [city]`; record search surface, location, language, date, and provider. Sample results are not universal visibility claims.
